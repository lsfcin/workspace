Course material Lucas has already produced (decks, scripts, activities) is an archive, not a draft.
The right contribution is to **interleave new slides between the existing ones and refine the
existing ones in place**. Never replace wholesale, never mark as skipped, never reorder what he
assembled.

**Why:** on 2026-08-19, in class 02 of Tecnologias na Educação, I pushed 25 new slides to the front
and marked his 56 as `isSkipped`. He answered: *"de fato descartou tudo do que tinha antes… tem como
aproveitar melhor os slides anteriores"*. The 56-slide deck carried years of cases, statistics and
examples I had not read in full — including the MODELO and the EXEMPLO I thought I was inventing.
Replacing threw away good work and erased his authorship.

**How to apply:** read the whole deck before proposing anything. Then an insertion plan by position
("four slides go in after slide 6") and a list of refinements by element. Emit the insertions **back
to front** so indices computed against the original deck stay valid within the same batch. When an
old slide repeats text that was refined, refine the copy too — inconsistency between slides is worse
than stale text. A new statistic goes in **with its source link on the slide itself**. If something
in the old material genuinely gets in the way, say which and why, and leave the removal to him. See
[[feedback-explore-before-cutting]] and [[feedback-visual-eyeball-gate]].
