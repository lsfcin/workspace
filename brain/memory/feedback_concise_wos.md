Stated 2026-08-24: the deliverable is a **more concise WOS** — reduce the overall number of lines rather than increase it. A session that adds a feature owes the reduction that pays for it.

**Why:** the workspace is read by agents at every session start, so every line is paid again and again. Growth nobody reads is a permanent tax. The roadmap's own "completion is deletion" rule was already this idea applied to one file; this generalises it to the whole tree.

**How to apply:** cut what repeats, what nobody reads, and what a generator can derive. Never summarize a rule until it stops being enforceable — where conciseness and enforceability pull against each other, say so and let Lucas rule instead of trimming a law to hit a number.
