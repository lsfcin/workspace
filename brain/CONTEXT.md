# Brain
> Personal OS: goals, attention, ideas, life. Agent collaborates here.

Not a productivity tool — a thinking-partner space, where the agent is a first-class collaborator rather than a reader.

**Lucas** — Lucas Silva Figueiredo, Várzea/Recife-PE. CS professor at UFRPE / CIn-UFPE, lab LIH.DD;
research in Hybrid Intelligence, Mechanism Design, AR and 3D Computer Vision. Vice-coordinator of Licenciatura em Computação at DC/UFRPE, leading the mandatory PPC revision (MEC CNE/CP 4/2024).
Creative, fuzzy thinker; a strong starter who struggles to finish. He is a **visual thinker** — for structure, a plan or a comparison, a diagram or a table beats paragraphs.

**Language.** Lucas may write pt-br anywhere. **`INBOX.md` is pt-br whoever writes it** (ruled 2026-08-28) — it is his diary, not an agent surface. The agent writes everything else in `brain/`, `core/`, `code/` and `academy/` in en-us.

Who Lucas is lives here, in the file every Brain task already opens, and nowhere else: a separate profile went unread for 88 sessions. His fears and dynamics are per-goal fields in [`goals/`](goals/CONTEXT.md); how to hold a conversation about them is [`../core/skills/compass.md`](../core/skills/compass.md).

**Capture is free, structure is earned.** A thought lands in `INBOX.md` with no taxonomy and no formatting, and `/inbox` routes it to a goal, a task, a reference — or deletes it. Nothing else here has to be tidy at write time. The routing table below says what each file is.

Goal file format, the five areas, section specs, signals/dynamics/timing fields, and the design rationale: [SPECS.md](SPECS.md).
